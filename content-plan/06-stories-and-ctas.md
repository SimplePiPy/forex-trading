# 06 — Stories, CTAs, and the Launch Sequences

Stories are where the relationship happens and where the mentorship sells. Reels bring strangers. Stories turn them into people who reply. Replies turn into calls.

## 1. The daily Story framework (10–15 Stories, three blocks)

### Block 1 · 6:30 AM · "Today" (3–5 Stories)
1. Face, truck or kitchen, 10 seconds: "Day X. Here's today." (Always the counter, top corner, as text.)
2. The plan: the job, the trees, or the beach, 1 photo + 1 line.
3. **Poll** ("Which tree first? A or B" / "Beach yoga or beach run?"). Polls are the cheapest engagement there is.
4. Optional: a screenshot of last night's best comment with your reply.
5. **CTA of the day** (see §2 rotation).

### Block 2 · 1:00 PM · "Work" (4–6 Stories)
1. POV clip, 10–15 seconds, raw, no edit.
2. A crew moment or a Mariah/beach moment (with consent).
3. One lesson, text on a photo: "Rule #[N]: the crew never waits on the client."
4. **Question box**: "Ask me anything about [today's topic]."
5. A reply to one of the questions (screenshot the question, answer on camera).
6. Reshare the day's Reel A with a line: "This one's doing [X] in 4 hours."

### Block 3 · 8:30 PM · "Numbers + Ask" (3–5 Stories)
1. Screenshot of the day's Insights or the follower count: "Day X. [number]."
2. A one-line honest reflection.
3. **The CTA** (rotation §2), always with a "reply with [emoji]" or a link sticker.
4. A member/waitlist screenshot when you have them ("[trade] from [state] just joined the waitlist").
5. "Tomorrow: [teaser]." Close the loop into tomorrow's Block 1.

Rules: face in at least 4 Stories a day. Talk like you're on the phone. Use the counter on every Story (a small text "Day 23/90" in the top corner; make it a saved sticker in your camera roll so it's 2 seconds).

## 2. The CTA rotation (one primary CTA per day, cycle through)

| Day of week | Primary CTA | Sticker/mechanic | Where it leads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | "DM me CLIMB" | Text + link sticker to Playbook | Playbook → list |
| Tuesday | "Reply with 🌲 if you're a tradesman" | Emoji reply | Human DM thread → you ask their trade |
| Wednesday | "Ask me anything about [today's topic]" | Question box | Replies → tomorrow's content + DM threads |
| Thursday | "Live tonight 8 PM: I'll fix your hook" | Countdown sticker | Live → clips → DMs |
| Friday | "Crew Member of the Week: [name]. Want to be next?" | Link to application | Application |
| Saturday | "Poll: what should I teach next?" | Poll | Engagement + content ideas |
| Sunday | "Playbook updated. Day X numbers inside." | Link sticker | Playbook → list → email |

Secondary CTA every day in Block 3: whatever is currently open (waitlist, Sprint, Climb, Cohort 2).

## 3. The twelve Story sequences

Each sequence is 6–10 Stories posted over 2–3 hours. Save them as Highlights named **"Day 1"**, **"The Job"**, **"Beach"**, **"Numbers"**, **"The Crew"**, **"Sprint"**, **"Climb"**.

### 3.1 · LAUNCH (Day 1)
1. Face, 6:30 AM: "Day 1 of 90. Watch this."
2. Text on a tree photo: "500,000 followers. 90 days. From up there."
3. Face: "Here's why: [the baby, the business, the tradesmen who deserve to be seen]."
4. Poll: "Think I'll make it? Yes / No / Don't care, I'm watching."
5. The Reel A reshared: "Day 1 is live."
6. Question box: "What do you want to see? Tree stuff, business stuff, or the quiet stuff?"
7. Evening: "Day 1 numbers: [X]." Face: "Thank you. See you at 7 AM."
8. "DM me CLIMB for the Playbook. It's blank right now. That's the point. We fill it together."

### 3.2 · WAITLIST (Day 14)
1. Face: "Two weeks in. [X] followers. I've had [N] tradesmen DM me asking how."
2. Text: "So here's what I'm going to do."
3. Face: "I'm going to take a handful of you and build it with you. Not a course. With you."
4. Text: "Details next week. If you want in early, reply with 🌲."
5. Screenshot of DMs (names blurred): "This is what my inbox looks like."
6. "Reply 🌲 and tell me your trade. I'll answer every one."
7. Evening: "[N] replies. This is going to be small on purpose."

### 3.3 · FOUNDING SPRINT LAUNCH (Day 21, 6 AM → 9 PM)
1. 6:00 AM, face: "It's Day 21. Here it is."
2. Text card: "THE 14-DAY SPRINT. 5 tradesmen. $1,500. 2 calls, 14 days of me in your DMs, 1 collab post on this account."
3. Face: "Who it's for: you own a trade business, you've got at least one good job you're proud of, and you're sick of being invisible."
4. Face: "Who it's not for: if you want a course to watch and not do, this isn't it."
5. Text: "Founding 5 get their name in the Playbook forever and the founding price, forever."
6. Link sticker: "Apply here. I read every one today."
7. 12:00: "2 seats gone. [Trade] from [state], [trade] from [state]."
8. 3:00: Q&A: answer the 3 most common questions on camera.
9. 8:30: "3 left. Closing when they're gone, not on a date."
10. "Thank you for being here on Day 21. Tomorrow, back up a tree."

### 3.4 · DAY 30 (milestone)
1. The Day 30 Reel reshared.
2. Face: "30 days. [X] followers. Here's what I thought would happen and what actually happened."
3. Graph screenshot.
4. Text: "Best post: [ ]. Worst: [ ]. Biggest lesson: [ ]."
5. Face: "The 5 Sprint guys, Day 9. Here's [name]'s first post that did [X]."
6. "The Climb (90 days, 10 people) opens Day 45. Waitlist: reply with 🧗."
7. Poll: "Should I show the money side of this? Yes / Keep it to the climb."
8. "Thank you. Day 31 at 7 AM."

### 3.5 · THE CLIMB LAUNCH (Day 45)
Same structure as 3.3, 12 Stories across the day. Additions: a text card for the Lead Board ("members send each other jobs, I take nothing"), a text card for the collab posts ("3 posts on this account"), a founding-price card ("$3,700 forever, $4,700 for everyone after Day 60"), and a live at 8 PM with the link sticker in the last Story.

### 3.6 · THE FLOP (any day a post underperforms)
1. Screenshot the post with its bad number. Face: "This one flopped."
2. "Here's what I think went wrong."
3. Poll: "Was it the hook or the topic?"
4. "Here's what I'm doing about it tomorrow."
This sequence builds more trust than any win.

### 3.7 · THE WIN (any day a post hits 3x average)
1. Screenshot: "This one's doing [X] in [hours]."
2. Face: "Here's the hook and why it worked."
3. "Sequel tomorrow at 7."
4. "DM HOOKS if you want the hook bank I'm pulling from."

### 3.8 · CREW FRIDAY
1. Member's before (screenshot). 2. Member's after. 3. Face: "Here's what he did." 4. "Want to be next? Link." 5. Reshare the member's post with a Collab tag.

### 3.9 · BEACH WEEK DAILY
1. Sunrise. 2. Counter + face: "Day X, still posting." 3. Something real (Mariah, food, the book). 4. The Operator lesson of the day, text card. 5. Evening numbers.

### 3.10 · LIVE NIGHT (Thursdays)
1. Morning: countdown sticker "Hook Clinic 8 PM." 2. Afternoon: "Send me your hook, I'll fix 5 live." 3. 7:45: "15 minutes." 4. After: 3 clips, "Missed it? Here's one."

### 3.11 · THE BABY (whenever, only what you both agree)
1. Face: one sentence. 2. A photo. 3. "Day X. Still here." Nothing else is owed.

### 3.12 · THE FINAL CLIMB (Days 85–90, three times a day)
1. Morning: counter + today's film reshared. 2. Midday: live count screenshot. 3. Evening: "Watch party 8 PM" countdown. 4. Post-live: "Tomorrow: [film title]."

## 4. Broadcast Channel cadence ("The Crew")

- Morning: one line. "Day 23. 7 AM post is a rigging cut that made me sweat. Go."
- Evening: the number. "Day 23: [X]. Best post today: [ ]."
- Sundays: "Playbook updated. [Link]."
- Launch days: the offer goes here **2 hours before** the Reel. Channel members always get first dibs. Say that out loud in the Reel: "The channel got this two hours ago."

## 5. The reply rules (DMs)

- Every keyword DM gets the automation, then a **human voice note** from you within 12 hours for anyone who answers the "what's your trade" question. Under 30 seconds. Use their name and their trade.
- Every 🌲 reply gets a real reply.
- Every question box answer that's good becomes tomorrow's Story or Slot C.
- Anyone who mentions money, jobs, or "how do I do this" gets the Sprint DM script (02 §4).
- 20 minutes in the morning, 20 at night, 20 at lunch. Set a timer. Then close the app.
