## The one you made by accident

**Clips 3 and 6.** You set up a camera to film a morning routine and instead documented yourself reaching for your phone twice inside two minutes. Once before you'd written a word, once in the middle of writing.

You didn't notice either time. You only know because the camera was running.

**That's the best post in this batch and it isn't close.** It's honest, it's universal, and the footage is the evidence. You wrote a script about the phone problem two days ago and then unintentionally filmed the proof.

**Cut:** the sit, the coffee, the first phone reach, opening the journal, the second phone reach. Do not cut around either one. Let both be in frame.

**Hook:** "Watch how many times I pick up my phone."

**Caption:**
> I set up a camera to film my morning. Coffee, journal, sunrise, the whole thing.
>
> Watched it back and I check my phone twice in under two minutes. Once before I've written a single word, and once in the middle of writing.
>
> I didn't notice either time. I only know because the camera was running.
>
> I'm not going to sit here and tell you I've fixed it. But you can't fix a thing you can't see, and now I've seen it.

**Pinned:** "Film your own morning sometime. You won't like it. 👇"

## TIER 1 · Post these first

### 1 · The houses I can't afford
**Clips:** 5, plus 17, 23, 25 for cutaways

> Did my stretches on a rented deck looking at houses I can't afford.
>
> That used to make me feel behind. Now I just run the math.
>
> A lot of those are second homes. I don't know anybody's life and I'm not going to pretend I do, but somebody's paying for a house they see a couple weeks a year, and they're somewhere else right now earning it.
>
> I'm not chasing the house. I'm chasing the couple of weeks becoming a couple of months.

**Hook:** "Doing yoga on a rented deck looking at houses I can't afford."

That last line is the post. Keep it exactly that length.

### 2 · Nobody posts the nap
**Clip:** 2

> Two in the afternoon on a Tuesday. I threw the remote across the bed and went to sleep.
>
> Everybody in this space posts five a.m. Nobody posts the nap.
>
> I climb trees for a living. My body *is* the business. A tired climber is a slow climber, and the slow one is the one who makes the mistake.
>
> Rest isn't the opposite of discipline. It's part of the equipment.

**Hook:** "Nobody in the discipline space posts the nap."

**Pinned:** "When's the last time you took a nap without feeling guilty? 👇"

Counterintuitive content in a saturated niche, and you're one of very few people with a real physical reason for it.

### 3 · Why I filmed so much wind
**Clips:** 12, 14, then 16, 17, 23 as cutaways

> Somebody asked me why I was filming so much wind this week.
>
> Because wind is the thing that decides whether I go up.
>
> Fifteen mile an hour gusts and I'm working. Twenty-five and I'm looking at the tree differently. Thirty-five and a dead limb sixty feet up starts coming down on its own schedule instead of mine.
>
> Everybody else at the beach sees a palm tree moving. I see the one variable I don't get a vote on.

**Hook:** "Why I filmed so much wind."

This turns five clips of ordinary grass into a post only you could make. It's also the best use of the footage you have the most of.

## TIER 2 · Solid, post over the next two weeks

**6 · The journal** (clip 1) — "One page, every morning, by hand." Pair with the first-hour script. Close, shallow, ocean soft behind you.

**7 · The bug** (clip 15) — "I've been looking at trees for twenty years, so I notice things nobody asks about." Bird chirping, then the bug, then the grass. An arborist noticing an insect is a genuinely different post from a nature account doing it. This also seeds your species and ecology authority.

**8 · The seashell** (clip 22) — shell, beach, shell, wave washes over. The cut is the whole idea. "The ocean takes it and puts it back a hundred times a day and the shell doesn't have an opinion about it."

**9 · The walk to the water** (13 into 18) — trail, grasses, then feet in sand, pan up to the water. Pair with the "an hour alone" script. This is your best footage for a voice-over piece.

**10 · The wave loop** (clip 21, 34 seconds) — cut a seamless 8 to 10 second loop, sound on, no voice, hook "Sound on." Loops with real audio outperform almost everything because people watch four times without meaning to.

**11 · Sunset** (clip 26) — the closer for any reflective voice-over. Or a 15-second silent post at the end of a hard week.

**12 · The sun through the clouds** (24) — birds chirping, light on the water. Pure Stillness. Ten seconds, one line of text, no CTA.

## What you didn't get, and it matters

Three of the highest-value shots on the camera list didn't happen:

- **You walking away from a locked-off camera, from behind.** That was the single most reusable clip on the list and you'll want it under a dozen voice-overs.
- **Hands, close and shallow.** Calluses in focus, background gone.
- **Rope.** Dock lines, a knot, anything. That was the shot that cuts invisibly into tree content.

All three are gettable at home or on the job this week in about ten minutes total. Get the walk-away first.
