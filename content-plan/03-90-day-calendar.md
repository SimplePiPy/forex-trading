# 03 — The 90-Day Calendar (day by day)

## How to read this

Every day has three Reel slots on Instagram (all times Eastern; re-tune after Week 2 using your Insights "most active times"):

- **Slot A · 7:00 AM · THE CLIMB** (reach): POV, tree work, danger, skill. Cross-post to TikTok + Shorts.
- **Slot B · 12:30 PM · SPEEDRUN or OPERATOR** (authority/buyers): numbers, lessons, business. Cross-post to TikTok + Shorts.
- **Slot C · 7:00 PM · STILLNESS or TRIAL** (retention/experiments): quiet content, or a Trial Reel testing a wild hook. Cross-post to TikTok.

Plus every day: 10–15 Stories in three blocks (see 06), 1 Broadcast Channel message, reply to every comment. Every other day: 1 carousel (saves). Sundays: YouTube long-form film at 9 AM + Playbook update + Sunday review.

Full scripts for Days 1–14 are in **05**. Hooks in **bold** below are the on-screen text for the first frame. Each entry names the keyword CTA to put in the caption and pinned comment.

Phases:
- **Days 1–5 · THE JOB** (Sep 14–18): finish the $40K job. Capture everything. Post the declaration.
- **Days 6–13 · THE BEACH** (Sep 19–26): Oak Island. Stillness arc + editing bank. Never break the counter.
- **Days 14–30 · THE ENGINE**: back on the tools, systems locked, waitlist, Founding Sprint opens Day 21, Day 30 film.
- **Days 31–60 · THE SCALE**: editor running, series formats locked, collabs, lives, Founding Climb opens Day 45, member proof begins.
- **Days 61–90 · THE COMPOUND**: Crew content, baby-prep arc, Cohort 2, the Final Climb (Days 85–90), Day 90 film.
