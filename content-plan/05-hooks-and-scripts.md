# 05 — Hooks, Series Formats, and the First 14 Days Scripted

## 1. Hook formulas that work for you (and why)

Every hook does one of five things in 2 seconds: shows something extreme, opens a loop, makes a claim, calls out a person, or promises a payoff. Combine visual + text.

| Formula | Pattern | Your version |
|---|---|---|
| **Extreme visual + number** | [insane thing] + [a number] | "2,000 lb limb, 60 feet up" |
| **Open loop** | "[Something happened] and I'll show you why" | "They said this one couldn't be climbed. Watch." |
| **The counter** | "Day X of 90…" | Always. It's the spine. |
| **Money** | "$[big number]…" | "$40,000 tree job. Here's where every dollar goes." |
| **Callout** | "If you're a [trade]…" / "Tag the guy who…" | "Tag your groundie." |
| **Contrast** | "[Loud thing] vs [quiet thing]" | "70 feet up yesterday. Ocean today." |
| **Confession** | "The mistake that cost me $X" | "The cut I still think about." |
| **Anti-hook** | "I wasn't going to post this." | Works on re-hooks of old footage. |
| **Proof** | "This post got [X]. Here's why." | Speedrun content. |
| **Fatherhood** | "Building this so my kid…" | Sparingly. Real. |

## 2. The eight repeatable series (lock these by Day 31)

A series lowers the cost of every post and trains the audience to come back.

1. **"Cuts You Can't Un-See"** (Climb, weekly): one cut, slow-mo, the sound, a single caption.
2. **"The Groundie's View"** (Climb, weekly): GoPro on a crew member. Underrated angle nobody posts.
3. **"Rope Walk, No Music"** (Climb, weekly): ASMR at height. Long watch time.
4. **"Storm Season"** (Climb, weekly): call-outs, 6 AM, the chaos.
5. **"Day X" recap** (Speedrun, weekly Sunday): numbers on screen, honest, Playbook link.
6. **"Crew Member of the Week"** (Speedrun/Proof, Fridays): a member's before/after.
7. **"Stump Meditation"** (Stillness, weekly): 15–30 seconds, guided, on a stump, at a job.
8. **"One Book, One Line"** (Stillness, Sundays): hold the book, read one line, one sentence on why.

Plus the Operator formats: **"Where the money goes"** (breakdowns), **"Rate my quote"** (comments bait), **"How I'd start [trade] with $X"** (evergreen, searchable).

## 4. The first 14 days, fully scripted

Format: **[on-screen hook]** → what the camera shows → what you say (VO = voice-over, TH = talking head) → last line → caption + CTA.

## 5. Re-hook rule

Every Reel that beats your 7-day average by 3x gets:
1. A **sequel** within 72 hours ("Part 2" is fine, "What happened next" is better).
2. A **re-hook** within 7 days (same footage, new first frame, new text).
3. A **carousel** version within 7 days (the lesson, in slides, for saves).
4. A **TikTok raw** version (longer, less cut).

That's 4 more shots at a proven idea. This rule alone is a third of your growth.
