# Day 2 · Tuesday, September 15 · The Story Sequence

**Reordered for the real day:** email already sent, gym now, call after, then house, plan, head.

**The thread running through all of it:** you had energy you couldn't sit still with, so you went and burned it off before making a call that matters. That's the story. Not "gym day." A man who knows he'd make a worse phone call at 9 AM than at 11 AM, and does something about it. Nobody in the trades posts that.

13 Stories, four blocks. Corner text **Day 2/90** on every single one. Make it a saved sticker so it's two taps.

---

## Block 2 · At and after the gym

**Story 4** · One clip mid-workout, 8 seconds, whatever you're on. Handheld, ugly, fine.

Don't film other people at the gym. Point it at yourself, the bar, the floor, the clock.

> "Nobody tells you how much of running a company is managing your own head so you don't wreck something."

**Story 6** · Selfie, in the truck after, sweaty, 15 seconds
> "That's better. Here's what I'm walking into. Progress payment on a forty-thousand-dollar job. I'm out of town for a while after this week. If this doesn't move today it sits for eight days and I'm chasing it in October. Eleven-minute phone call. Making it now."

**Story 7** · Countdown or just text:
> "Calling in 5. I'll tell you how it goes either way."

That promise is what makes people come back to your Stories in an hour. Keep it.

## Block 3 · After the call

**Story 8** · Selfie, 20 seconds, whatever actually happened. Three versions depending on the outcome.

*If it went well:*
> "Done. [ACH / check pickup / wire], they're processing it [day]. Here's what worked: I didn't email and wait. I called the person who signed the contract, not accounting, and I asked her one question. 'What's the soonest you've ever turned one of these around?' Not 'can you hurry.' What's possible. People answer that question honestly."

*If it's slow but moving:*
> "Alright. It's in their system, it goes to accounting [day], and I've got a name and a direct line. Not what I wanted but it's not sitting in a void anymore. That's the difference between an email and a phone call."

*If it went badly:*
> "Didn't get what I wanted. [One line on what happened.] Here's what I'm doing about it: [the next step]. I said I'd tell you either way. This is the either way."

The third version is the most valuable one you will ever post. If it goes badly, post it.

**Story 9** · Text on a plain background:
> "Rule: never let a payment sit over a vacation. You will not fix it from the beach and you will think about it the whole time."

**Story 10** · Question box
> "What's the invoice you've been avoiding chasing?"

Screenshot the best answers tonight or tomorrow and reply on camera. That's free content and those people become DMs.

## The rules for today's Stories

- **Corner counter on every one.** Day 2/90. Never skip it.
- **Face in at least 5 of the 13.** Text-only Stories are filler between the real ones.
- **Talk like you're on the phone with a friend.** No performing. You're tired and revved up and that's the content.
- **Keep the promise in Story 7.** If you say you'll report back, report back. That's the habit that makes people watch all 13 tomorrow.
- **Never name the client, the park, or show an invoice with their details.** You're still owed money.
- **Reply to every Story reply.** A Story reply is a DM thread, and a DM thread is how the mentorship gets sold in three weeks.

## If the day goes sideways

If the call doesn't happen, the house doesn't get cleaned, or you just don't have it in you: post Stories 1, 6, and 13 and nothing else. Three Stories and three Reels still counts. The streak is the strategy.
