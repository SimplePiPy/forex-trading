## Setup (2 minutes)
- iPhone, vertical, 4K/30, Cinematic mode OFF. Prop it on the coffee table or a stack of books at eye level, arm's length away, or hand it to Mariah.
- Sit on the couch. Lamp on, behind the phone, not behind you. TV off.

- Have the helmet or the saw or the rope in frame, on the couch next to you or on the floor. That's the whole visual: a tree guy on a couch with a helmet.
- Record 60 seconds. It'll cut to 35–40.

## The script (talk like you're on the phone with a friend, not reading)

> Tomorrow morning at 7 I start something stupid.
>
> I run a tree company. I climb for a living. Tomorrow I've got a forty-thousand-dollar job to finish at a mobile home park, and I'm going to post three times a day from the top of it. Every day. For ninety days. Until I hit five hundred thousand followers, or until I show you every number on the way to why I didn't.
>
> Here's why. [Pick up the helmet, or just glance at it.] I've been doing work nobody sees for a long time. Most tradesmen have. The best climber in your town has forty followers and a full voicemail. I built a company that runs without me standing on the job every day. Now I'm going to build an audience the same way, in public, and write down everything that works so any guy with a truck can copy it.
>
> There's one more reason. I'm not saying it yet. Some things you tell your family before you tell the internet. Give me two weeks.
>
> Next Saturday I'll be at the beach. I'm still posting. Day one is tomorrow at 7 AM.
>
> If you've ever done work that nobody sees, stick around. Day zero.

## Cut notes
- First frame: you already mid-sentence on "stupid," text on screen. Cut the breath before it.
- Hard cut after "why I didn't." No pause.
- "Give me two weeks." Let that one sit for half a second before the cut. It's the loop that brings people back on Day 14.
- End on "Day zero." then one second of you looking at the camera, then cut to black with **"Day 0 / 90"** in text. That end card is the loop point for every post after this.
