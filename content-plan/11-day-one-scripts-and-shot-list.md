## What you can and can't shoot on an iPhone today

**Do:**
- Ground shots, handheld. The iPhone 15 Pro Max is a better camera than most people's whole rig.
- The look-up from the base of the tree, tilting up to you in the canopy. A groundie can hold it.
- The drop, filmed wide and static from a safe distance. Brace the phone on the truck bed rail. Hold 10 seconds after it lands.
- The crew, the chipper, the brush, the gear on the tailgate.
- Your face, any time you're on the ground.

**Do not:**
- Climb with the phone in your hand or in a pocket you'd reach for.
- Tape, zip-tie, or strap the phone to your helmet, your saddle, or a rope. An improvised mount 60 feet up is a dropped phone at best and a distraction that costs you a hand at worst. The POV content is worth a lot. It is not worth that. It waits for the GoPro and a real mount tomorrow.

Today is a ground-footage day. Tomorrow you're back in the canopy with the helmet cam.
