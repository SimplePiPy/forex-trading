# Beach Week · Days 6–13 · The Archive + The Plan

**The principle:** you are not going to film every day. You're going to film **twice**, on one day, and the whole week is done. Slot A comes from the bank you already shot. That's the entire point of the week, and it's also the exact lesson you're teaching.

**Budget:** 90 minutes of batch shooting one morning, 30 minutes of posting and replies each day. That's it. Everything else is vacation.

**Opsec, still on:** never a date, never "I'm at the beach right now" in real time. Post on a **one-day delay**. Shoot Sunday morning, post Monday. Nobody can tell. No house exterior, no street, nothing that says where the rental is.

**Embargo, still on:** nothing about the pregnancy until after you've told your parents and the Day 14 post goes up.

---

## PART 1 · The Beach Archive (what to capture)

### Batch A · One sunrise, 45 minutes, iPhone on a tripod or propped

Sunrise at Oak Island this week is right around 7:00. Be set up by 6:40. Pick the clearest morning, probably your second or third day once you've slept.

Shoot all of these in one go. Wide, you small in the frame, the water big. Don't perform. Each one is 2 minutes of recording that becomes 15 seconds.

| # | Shot | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sitting on the sand, eyes closed, back to camera or profile | Stillness Slot C |
| 2 | Yoga or stretching, wide, silhouette against the light | Slot C |
| 3 | Walking away from camera down the empty beach | Slot C, and B-roll for everything |
| 4 | Writing in the notebook, coffee beside you, close on hands | Slot C |
| 5 | Reading, holding the book so the cover reads | Slot C |
| 6 | Close details: feet in wet sand, hands, the horizon | B-roll |
| 7 | Three 20-second static holds of just water and sky, no people | B-roll under every voice-over this week |
| 8 | One sunrise timelapse, phone locked off, 10 minutes | Slot C or the Sunday film opener |

That's eight pieces of Slot C from one morning. You need six.

### Batch B · Same day, 30 minutes, six talking heads

Porch, truck, or standing on the sand. **Change your shirt between each one** so they don't look like one session. DJI mic on.

1. "Five systems that let me leave my company for a week."
2. "How to quote a tree job in 3 minutes." Screen record the sheet separately.
3. "Why most tree guys stay broke, and it isn't the work."
4. "The mistake that cost me $[X]. Don't do this."
5. "How I'd start a tree company with $10K today."
6. "The week that almost broke the schedule." The lift showing up six hours late with no fluid, the crane, two crews, what it cost, what you'd do differently.

Say each one twice. Take the second take, it's always better.

### Ongoing, incidental, no effort

- Food, the deck, coffee, the walk. Shoot it, use it in Stories.
- Your parents and anyone else: **ask first, once, plainly.** Take the answer and don't ask again.
- Anything that makes you feel like you got away with something. That's the Operator story.

Slot A comes from the lift and crane bank every single day. Slot B and C come from the batches. You're posting, not producing.

## PART 3 · Two changes to what comes next

**1 · The Day 14 reveal and the waitlist should not share a day.**

Day 14 was going to be both the pregnancy reveal and the waitlist opening. Don't do that. One is the most personal thing you'll post all quarter and the other is commercial. Putting them together cheapens the first and buries the second.

- **Day 14, Sunday** The reveal, plus the Week 2 recap. Nothing being sold.
- **Day 15, Monday** The waitlist. "Something's coming for the operators in here. Reply 🌲."

Everything downstream still works. The Sprint still opens Day 21.

**2 · Film the reveal at the beach, after you tell them.**

Shoot it while you're still there, the day after the conversation, with the water behind you. It'll look and feel completely different from a truck-cab talking head, and it's a post you only get to make once. Hold it until Day 14.

## PART 4 · The 30-minute daily routine

Set a timer. When it goes off, put the phone down.

- **7:00** Post A. Reply for 10 minutes.
- **12:30** Post B. Reply for 10 minutes. Story block.
- **7:00 PM** Post C. Reply for 10 minutes. Story block with the day's number.
- **Broadcast Channel** one line in the morning, the number at night.

**If you skip a day of replies, fine. Never skip a post.** The bank exists so that posting costs you four minutes.
