Why these times: 7 AM catches tradesmen before work and commuters; 12:30 is lunch; 7 PM is the evening scroll. After 14 days, open Insights → Audience → Most Active Times and shift each slot to the nearest peak. TikTok and Shorts can post at the same times; they're worldwide anyway.

Cross-posting rules: export from CapCut without any watermark. Re-upload natively to each platform (never share the Instagram link to TikTok). Slightly different first-line caption per platform. Same hook text on screen.

## 3. Gear rigs by situation

### In the tree (Slot A footage)
- **GoPro Hero 11 Black on the helmet**, front mount, angled slightly down so the saw and your hands are in frame. 4K/60 for slow-mo drops, 2.7K/120 if you want real slow motion on a big fall. HyperSmooth on. Horizon lock on. Wide, not SuperView (SuperView warps the edges and looks cheap in 9:16 crops).
- **Linear + Horizon Lock** field of view for anything you'll crop vertical.
- Record continuously. A 128GB card gets ~3 hours at 4K/60. Carry 3 cards. Swap at lunch.
- **Audio:** the GoPro's own mic in a wind sleeve is fine for saw sound. For voice in the tree, clip a **DJI Mic transmitter** under the collar, receiver on the GoPro via the Media Mod or just record the DJI transmitter's onboard backup and sync later.
- Rule: **film the whole climb every day.** You don't know which cut will be the 5M one until it happens. Storage is cheap. Missed moments are expensive.

### On the ground (Slot A B-roll, crew, cinematic)
- **Sony ZV-E10 on the gimbal.** 4K/30 or 1080/120 for slow-mo. Product Showcase off. Face-priority AF on. Shoot **vertical** with the camera rotated on the gimbal for anything meant for Reels; shoot horizontal for the YouTube film.
- Whoever's on the ground (Mariah before the baby, or a groundie for 15 minutes at lunch) gets a **shot list** taped to the truck:
  1. The look-up: camera at the base, tilt up to you in the tree (5 sec)
  2. The drop: wide, static, the limb falls through frame (hold 10 sec)
  3. The crew: slow push-in on the groundies dragging brush (10 sec)
  4. The saw: close-up of the bar cutting, sawdust (5 sec)
  5. The hero: you walking to the truck, helmet off, low angle (10 sec)
  6. The check/handshake/tailgate at the end (10 sec)
  7. Anything that scares them
- **Second GoPro** if you can borrow one: strap it to the rigging line or a chest mount on the groundie for "The Groundie's View."

### Talking head (Slot B)
- **iPhone 15 Pro Max**, 4K/30, Cinematic mode OFF, in the truck or standing at the job with the crew moving behind you. Camera at eye level, arm's length, slightly above. Sun behind the camera, not behind you.
- **DJI Mic** lav clipped to the collar, receiver in the Lightning/USB-C port. Never use the phone's mic for a talking head outdoors.
- Say the hook first, then say it again slightly differently. The editor uses the better take.
- 45–60 seconds max on the record. Cut to 30–40.

### Stillness (Slot C)
- ZV-E10 on a tripod, wide, you small in frame, environment big. Or the iPhone on a mini tripod for the writing/reading close-ups.
- Natural sound. Wind sleeve on. No music in the raw; the editor adds it (or not).
- Beach week: sunrise is 7:00–7:10 AM at Oak Island that week. Be set up by 6:40.

### Stories
- **iPhone, native camera, no stabilizer.** Rough is right for Stories. Vertical. Face + place. Talk like you're on the phone with a friend.

## 4. Edit pipeline (CapCut desktop, or hand it to the editor)

1. **Ingest** nightly: GoPro cards + ZV-E10 card + iPhone AirDrop → Google Drive folder `/footage/DayXX/`. Name the best 3 moments in a text file: `DayXX_notes.txt` ("2:14 on card 2, big oak drop, use this").
2. **Cut order:** hook frame first. The first frame is either the most extreme visual (the drop, the height) or the hook text on a clean frame. Never a logo, never a slate, never you saying "hey guys."
3. **Text:** hook text in the top-safe zone, big, 3–8 words, white with a black stroke, or the Instagram native "Classic" font. Captions burned in, mid-lower third, safe from the UI overlays.
4. **Length:** Climb 12–45 s. Speedrun/Operator 30–50 s. Stillness 15–25 s. Compilations 20–30 s. The Day 30/60/90 films 60–90 s.
5. **Loop:** for anything under 20 s, cut so the last frame leads into the first (the limb hits the ground → cut → the limb is back on the tree).
6. **Sound:** trending audio at 5–10% under the natural sound, or none. Saw noise is the audio.
7. **Export:** 1080×1920, 30 or 60 fps, H.264, high bitrate. No watermark.
8. **Cover:** pick a frame with your face or the height. Add the hook text. The grid matters when people click through from a viral post.
9. **Caption:** first line repeats the hook. Then 2–3 lines of story. Then: "Comment CLIMB and I'll send you the Playbook." Then 3–5 hashtags (#treework #arborist #treeclimbing #bluecollar #smallbusiness, rotate).
10. **Pinned comment**, posted within 30 seconds of publishing: the CTA again, plus a question.

## 5. Hire the editor (do this Day 8–10, from the beach)

You cannot cut 21 posts a week, run a tree company, prep for a baby and reply to 300 comments a day. The $40K job pays for this.

- **Budget:** $800–$2,000/month for 21 short cuts a week + 1 long-form. Philippines, Pakistan, Eastern Europe, or a US college kid who already edits for tree/hunting/trades channels.
- **Where:** Post in the Jun Yu community if there's one, Twitter/X "looking for a short-form editor" with 3 of your posts attached, Upwork with a 48-hour test, or DM editors credited on tree-content accounts you admire.
- **The test:** send 20 minutes of raw footage + the hook. Pay $30 for one Reel. Hire whoever makes you say "I'd post that" without changes.
- **The SOP** (put it in Notion, share it):
  - Daily: 3 cuts delivered by 10 PM the night before, in Drive `/ready/DayXX/`
  - Style guide: this section, plus 5 reference Reels from your own account
  - Hook text comes from you (the Sunday script sheet), never invented by the editor
  - Sunday: long-form film cut from the week's best, 12–18 minutes, chaptered
  - Turnaround on a "this one hit, cut 2 sequels" request: 12 hours
- **Retainer, not per-video.** Per-video editors slow down when you speed up.

## 6. The Job Week (Days 1–5, Sep 14–18): capture plan

This is the most valuable five days of footage you will have all quarter. Treat it like a film shoot that also happens to be a tree job.

- **Night before:** cards formatted, batteries charged (GoPro dies in 60–80 min at 4K/60; carry 4 batteries and a power bank in the truck), helmet mount tightened, DJI mics charged and paired, ZV-E10 gimbal balanced.
- **Every morning (6:30):** 20-second tailgate talking head: "Day X. Here's the plan for today." That's Story block 1 and potentially a Reel.
- **All day:** GoPro rolling every climb. Groundie or Mariah runs the ZV-E10 shot list for 15 minutes in the morning, 15 at lunch, 15 at the end. Phone in chest pocket for 5–8 Stories.
- **Lunch (10 min):** film Slot B talking head for tomorrow. One take, on the tailgate.
- **End of day:** the "what happened today" 30-second talking head + the check/progress moment if there is one.
- **Night (60–90 min):** ingest, notes file, cut tomorrow's A from today's best. Post C for today if not done.
- **Friday, Day 5:** get the completion moment on camera. The last cut, the last drag, the handshake, the invoice, the drive away. This is the ending of YouTube film #1 and the Day 30 film.

## 7. The Beach Week (Days 6–13, Sep 19–26): the plan that keeps the counter alive

**Principle:** you're not "posting on vacation." You're documenting that a business owner with systems can leave. That's Operator content. Mariah gets a real vacation; you spend 90 minutes each morning and 30 minutes at night on this, and the rest is off.

- **Before you leave (Day 5 night):** pick the 8 best Climb clips from the job week, note them for the editor (or rough-cut them yourself Friday night). Slot A for the entire week is covered.
- **Morning (6:40–8:10):** sunrise stillness shot (yoga, meditation, writing) on the tripod = Slot C. Then 10 minutes for a Slot B talking head on the porch or the beach. Then post A, reply.
- **Midday:** post B, 15 minutes of replies, Story block 2 (beach, book, food, a lesson). Then be on vacation.
- **Evening (8:30–9:00):** post C, replies, Story block 3 with the day's number.
- **Days 8–10:** hire the editor (§5). This is a beach-chair task.
- **Day 13 drive home:** record the "what beach week taught me" talking head in the car, and the AMA Slot C.
- Ask Mariah once, clearly, what she wants on camera and what she doesn't. Write it down. Never revisit it in the moment.

## 8. When the baby comes

Whenever it happens, it happens. The plan is:
- Editor has a **7-day emergency bank** of Slot A and C cuts built by Day 40. Keep it topped up.
- Slot B for that week is pre-recorded Sunday.
- One Story, when you're ready, in your own words. One Reel, when you're ready: "Day X. They're here." Nothing else is owed to anyone.
- Crew Call that week is run by a Sprint alumnus you've picked in advance.
