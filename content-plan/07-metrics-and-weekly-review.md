# 07 — Metrics, the Sunday Review, and the Kill/Double-Down Rules

## 1. Daily log (2 minutes, a Google Sheet)

Columns: Date · Day # · Followers (IG) · Followers (TikTok) · Subs (YT) · Post A views/follows · Post B views/follows · Post C views/follows · Sends on best post · Keyword DMs today · Playbook signups · Applications · Sales · Revenue · Note (one line: what happened).

Instagram "follows from this post" is in each Reel's Insights. Track it. Views are vanity; follows-per-view is the number that tells you which hook converts strangers.

## 2. Benchmarks to watch (and what to do)

| Metric | Healthy | Action if below |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3-second retention) | >70% | The first frame is wrong. Re-hook. |
| Average watch % | >60% on <20 s, >40% on 30–50 s | Cut the middle. Move the payoff earlier. |
| Sends per reach | >1% cold | Add a "send this to…" line. Make the idea tribal. |
| Follows per 1,000 views | >8 | Profile/bio isn't converting the spike. Fix the pinned comment, the cover, the bio. |
| Keyword DMs per day | 20+ by Day 14, 100+ by Day 45 | CTA isn't in the pinned comment or the caption's first lines. |
| Playbook signups / DMs | >40% | The auto-DM is too long. One line, one link, one question. |
| Application / signups | >5% during launches | The offer story isn't clear. Re-cut the offer Reel. Use the member proof. |
| Close rate on calls | >40% | You're pitching. Ask more questions. Use the 6-step call. |

## 3. Sunday review (60 minutes, every week, non-negotiable)

1. **Pull the week's numbers** into the sheet. Top 3 posts and bottom 3 posts by follows-per-view.
2. **Why did the top 3 work?** Hook type, pillar, length, sound, time. Write one sentence each.
3. **Why did the bottom 3 flop?** Same. Be honest. Usually: first frame was you talking, or two ideas in one post, or no payoff.
4. **Double-down list:** each top-3 post gets a sequel, a re-hook, and a carousel scheduled this week (05 §5).
5. **Kill list:** any series format with 3 straight below-average weeks gets cut or reinvented.
6. **Update the Playbook** (02 §2): numbers, the 5 that hit, the 5 that flopped, this week's lesson.
7. **Write the week:** 21 hooks in the script sheet, one line of "what the camera shows" each.
8. **Film the 7 Slot-B talking heads** in one block.
9. **Send the Sunday email + Broadcast message.**
10. **Check the funnel:** DM automation still firing? Link in bio correct? Day counter updated? Seats left correct on every Story highlight?

## 4. The double-down rule (the actual growth engine)

Any post ≥3x your trailing 7-day average follows-per-post:
- Sequel within 72 hours
- Re-hook within 7 days
- Carousel within 7 days
- Long-form segment in that Sunday's YouTube film
- Story sequence 3.7 the same day

Any post ≥10x average:
- All of the above, plus: pin it to the profile, change the cover of the next 3 posts to match its visual style, run it as a paid "boost" for $50–100 to lookalikes of your followers (only on posts that already proved organic; boosting flops is burning money), and mention it in every Slot B that week.

## 6. Kill criteria (things that quietly ruin speedruns)

- **Posting less than 3/day for more than 2 days in a row.** The system is the growth. Fix the bank, hire help, don't skip.
- **A post that opens on you saying "hey guys."** Delete the first 2 seconds. Every time.
- **Two ideas in one Reel.** Split it.
- **Reposting with a TikTok watermark.** Never.
- **Replying to comments 6 hours late.** The first hour is where distribution is decided.
- **Selling before Day 21.** You'll cap your reach and look like everyone else.
- **Not selling after Day 21.** Followers without an offer is a hobby.
- **Firearms as the visual subject on the main account.** It's a restricted category on Meta and it's the easiest way to get quietly throttled.
- **Changing the day counter format.** It's the brand. Keep it identical.
- **Skipping the Sunday review.** That's where the compounding happens.
