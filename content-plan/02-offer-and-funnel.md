# 02 — Offers, Funnel, Sales, and The Crew Network

## 1. The two offers

### THE 14-DAY SPRINT — $1,500
**For:** an owner or tradesman who wants their content engine built *with* them, fast.
**What they get:**
- 2 × 60-minute 1:1 calls (Day 1: positioning + hooks + their first 30 post ideas; Day 14: review and next 30 days)
- Their bio, profile, keyword automation, and Playbook installed
- Daily async feedback in a private DM thread for 14 days (voice notes fine)
- 1 collab post on your account (their best Reel, co-authored)
- 14-day access to the Crew community
**Promise:** "In 14 days you'll have a profile that converts, a system that produces a post a day without you thinking, and one post that's already reached more people than your whole account did last year."
**Capacity:** 5 at a time. Never more. Scarcity is real, not fake.

### THE 90-DAY CLIMB — $3,700 (or 3 × $1,333)
**For:** owners who want the whole thing: brand, system, buyers, and the network.
**What they get:**
- Everything in the Sprint, plus:
- Weekly group "Crew Call" (60 min, Tuesdays 7 PM ET) for 12 weeks. Hot seats, numbers review, hook clinic.
- Monthly 1:1 (3 total)
- 3 collab posts on your account over 90 days
- The Crew Lead Board (members pass each other jobs and referrals, see §6)
- Lifetime community access
- The full Speedrun Playbook, systems, templates, editor hiring SOP, quoting/collection scripts from your business
**Promise:** "In 90 days you'll have a brand that gets you the jobs you want, a content system that runs on 5 hours a week, a network of operators who send you work, and at least one post seen by a million people."
**Capacity:** 10 per cohort. Cohort 1 opens Day 45. Cohort 2 opens Day 75.

Upsell path: Sprint clients get the $1,500 credited toward the Climb if they upgrade within 30 days (they pay $2,200 more). Most will.

## 2. The funnel (simple on purpose)

```
Reel / TikTok / Short

   │  (link)
   ▼
Speedrun Playbook page  →  name + email + phone + "what's your trade / what's your goal"
   │

   ├──► Email/SMS list (2/week + launch sequences)
   │
   ▼
Application (short form, 6 questions)  →  you reply by DM voice note within 12 hours
   │
   ▼
20-minute call (or DM close for Sprint)  →  Stripe link  →  onboarding
```

### The bio (copy/paste)

> **[Name]** · Climber · Operator · Teacher
> Day X/90 of speed-running 0 → 500K 🌲
> Turning tradesmen into brands. Baby due [month].
> DM **CLIMB** for the free Speedrun Playbook 👇
> [link]

Update the "Day X" every morning. People screenshot it.

### Keyword automation (ManyChat or Instagram's native "comment to DM")

Keywords and what they send:

| Keyword | Where it's used | Auto DM |
|---|---|---|
| **CLIMB** | Every post's caption/pinned comment | Playbook link + "What's your trade?" |
| **HOOKS** | Speedrun posts about hooks | The 120-hook bank (a Notion page) + Playbook link |
| **QUOTE** | Operator posts about the $40K job | Your quoting template PDF + Playbook link |
| **CREW** | Stories and launch posts | Application link |
| **STILL** | Stillness posts | Your 10-minute pre-climb meditation audio + Playbook link |

Every auto DM ends with a question, because a reply moves the conversation into a human thread and Instagram treats an active DM as a strong relationship signal.

### The Playbook (the lead magnet)

A single Notion page (or Google Doc) titled **"The 90-Day Speedrun Playbook (updated every Sunday)."**
Contents:
1. The current numbers (a screenshot of your insights, updated weekly)
2. The 4 pillars and the daily 3-slot schedule
3. The hook formulas that are working *this week*
4. The 5 posts that hit hardest and why
5. The 5 that flopped and why
6. This week's lesson
7. "Want it done with you?" → application link

The living document is the trick. It gives people a reason to come back, and it gives you a reason to email them every Sunday: "Playbook updated. Day 35 numbers inside."

## 4. DM close script (Sprint)

You'll get DMs like "how do I do what you're doing?" Here's the thread.

**You (voice note, under 60 seconds):** "Hey [name], saw you're a [trade]. Quick question so I don't waste your time: are you trying to get more/better jobs, or are you trying to build something beyond the truck? Both is fine, just tell me which one's louder."

**Them:** [answer]

**You:** "Got it. Here's what I'd do in your spot: [one specific, real suggestion about their account, e.g. 'your first frame is always your logo, kill that and start on the cut']. That's free. If you want me to build the whole engine with you over 14 days, it's $1,500, I take 5 people at a time, and there's [X] seats open right now. I do two calls with you, I'm in your DMs every day, and I collab-post your best Reel on my page. Want the details or want to just start?"

**Objection: "Let me think about it."**
"Totally. One thing: the seats are real, not marketing. When the 5 are gone I close it till the next round. Want me to hold one for 24 hours?"

**Objection: "$1,500 is a lot."**
"For a [trade] it's less than one job. The point is you never pay for a lead again. But if it's not the right time, grab the Playbook and run it yourself, I'll cheer you on. Tell me when you post your first one."

Never chase more than twice. The account does the chasing.

## 5. Call script (90-Day Climb, 20 minutes)

1. **(2 min) Where are you now?** Trade, revenue ballpark, followers, what they've tried.
2. **(3 min) Where do you want to be in 90 days?** Get a number: jobs, dollars, followers. Write it down. Say it back.
3. **(5 min) What's actually in the way?** They'll say time, camera shyness, not knowing what to post. Reflect it: "So the problem isn't the trade, it's the system."
4. **(5 min) The Climb, in their words.** "Here's what 90 days looks like for a [trade] at your size…" Use one member/example. Mention the collab posts and the Lead Board. Those are the two things nobody else offers.
5. **(3 min) Price and decision.** "$3,700 or three payments of $1,333. Ten seats. It's a yes, a no, or a not-now. All three are fine. Which one is it?"
6. **(2 min) Next steps.** Stripe link on the call. Onboarding call booked before you hang up.

## 6. The Crew: the network mechanic (this is the part nobody else does)

**Principle:** The mentor's audience is shared with the members. Members share leads with each other. Growth compounds inside the group.

### Mechanics
1. **Collab posts.** Every Sprint member gets 1, every Climb member gets 3. Instagram Collab posts appear on both profiles and pool the reach. At 100K+ followers, one collab is worth more to a 1,200-follower concrete guy than any course.
2. **The Crew Lead Board.** A shared sheet: trade, region, job type, "I have a lead for a [roofer] in [city]." Tree guys meet roofers meet landscapers. Referral fees agreed between members (suggest 10%). You take nothing. That generosity is the story.
3. **Crew Comments.** When a member posts, the Crew is notified (a Broadcast Channel just for members) and everyone leaves a real comment in the first 30 minutes. Not a pod, a crew.
4. **Member Spotlights.** Every Friday, a Reel on your account: "Crew member of the week: [name], [trade]. Day 1 he had 400 followers. Here's what he did." Their growth becomes your proof.
5. **Bring-a-Brother.** Any member who refers a paying member gets a free month of the community after their program ends, plus $300 credit toward their next program. Members recruit members.
6. **Crew Climb Day (Day 80).** In-person or virtual. One day, everyone posts the same series format at the same time, tagged. A wave on the algorithm. Shot for the Day 90 film.

### Why people stay after the program ends
- Lifetime community access
- The Lead Board keeps producing jobs
- Alumni get a collab post per quarter if they keep posting
- Alumni get first dibs on future cohorts to bring their crew
