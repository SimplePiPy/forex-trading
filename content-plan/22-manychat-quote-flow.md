# ManyChat Flow · The QUOTE Keyword

Set this up **before** the post goes live. A comment that gets no DM is a lead you paid for with a post and then threw away.

---

## 1 · What you need first

- [ ] The quote sheet uploaded to Google Drive, opened as a Google Sheet, shared **Anyone with the link → Viewer**
- [ ] The link shortened or at least copied somewhere you can paste it
- [ ] ManyChat connected to the Instagram account, on a plan that includes the Instagram comment trigger
- [ ] Instagram: Settings → Privacy → Messages → allow access to messages from people who comment

**On the Google Sheet link:** set it to Viewer, not Editor. If you leave it on Editor, the first person who opens it can overwrite the template for everybody. To let people take their own copy, share the normal view link and tell them to use File → Make a copy. Some people paste `/copy` at the end of the URL in place of `/edit` so it opens straight to the copy prompt. That's a nice touch, not a requirement.

---

## 2 · The trigger

**Type:** Instagram comment reply
**Post:** the "5 systems" Reel specifically, not all posts. Keep a separate trigger per post so you can see which post generates which leads.
**Keywords:** `QUOTE`, `quote`, `Quote`, `QOUTE`, `qoute`

That last pair matters. People typo it constantly and a missed keyword is a missed lead. Set the match to "contains" rather than "is exactly," so "quote please" and "QUOTE 🙏" both fire.

**Also set up, separately:** `CLIMB` for the Playbook and `STILL` for the pre-climb audio. Same structure, different link.

---

## 3 · The public comment reply

ManyChat will post a visible reply under their comment. **Write four variations and let it rotate.** Instagram gets suspicious of the same string posted a hundred times, and identical replies look botted to real humans reading the comments.

1. `Sent it 📩`
2. `Check your DMs 📩`
3. `On the way 📩`
4. `Just sent it over 📩`

Keep them this short. The public reply is a receipt, not a conversation.

---

## 4 · The DM sequence

### Message 1 · immediately

> Here it is 👇
>
> This is the actual sheet I price tree jobs with. Four numbers: man hours, access, what's underneath it, and debris. Fill in the yellow cells, it does the rest.
>
> Want me to send the link?

**Button:** `Send it over` → goes to Message 2

**Why gate the link behind a button.** It turns a one-way blast into a two-way conversation, which is what keeps the Instagram messaging window open and what the platform reads as a real interaction. It also filters. Somebody who won't tap a button was never going to open a spreadsheet.

### Message 2 · on button tap

> Here you go: [LINK]
>
> Two things I'd tell you before you use it.
>
> Use your LOADED labor rate, not the wage. Wage plus payroll tax plus comp plus benefits. For most tree companies that's 1.4x to 1.9x what the guy takes home.
>
> And run it against three jobs you've already finished and know the real cost of. Adjust it until it lands on what those jobs should have been. Then trust it.
>
> That's it. It's free, no email, nothing to sign up for.

### Message 3 · 45 seconds after Message 2

> One question so I know who I'm talking to, and then I'll leave you alone.
>
> What's your trade?

**Quick reply buttons:** `Tree work` · `Roofing` · `Concrete` · `Something else`

**Never ask before you deliver.** You do not hold a free thing hostage for an answer. Give it, then ask. People answer far more often that way and they answer honestly.

### Message 4 · on any answer

Set a tag for the trade they picked, then:

> Appreciate it. If you run that sheet on a job this week, DM me the number it spits out and I'll tell you if I think it's low.
>
> I'm on day [X] of a 90-day run to 500k. Everything I learn goes in the Playbook and it's free too. Just say CLIMB if you want it.

That last offer is the bridge from the tool to the relationship, and it costs nothing.

### Message 5 · 24 hours later, only for people tagged as a trade

> Did you get a chance to run it?
>
> Genuinely curious what number it gave you vs what you'd have quoted off the top of your head. That gap is the whole thing.

This message is the one that turns a lead into a conversation, and a conversation is what you'll sell the Sprint into on Day 21. Do not skip it.

## 5 · Tags and fields to create now

| Tag | Set when | You use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `quote-sheet-sent` | Message 2 delivered | Never send the same person the same thing twice |
| `trade-tree` | They answer tree work | Your highest-value segment |
| `trade-roofing` | They answer roofing | |
| `trade-concrete` | They answer concrete | |
| `trade-other` | Something else | |
| `replied-human` | They send any free-text message | These are your real leads. Watch this tag. |
| `sprint-waitlist` | They ask about working with you | Day 21 launch list |

**Custom field:** `first_keyword` — which keyword brought them in. In four weeks that tells you which post type actually produces buyers, which is worth more than any follower count.

---

## 6 · Notify yourself

Set a ManyChat notification to your email or the ManyChat mobile app whenever someone gets tagged `replied-human`. That is a real person typing a real sentence to you, and a voice note back within a few hours is the single highest-converting thing you can do all week.

Do not automate the reply to those. The automation gets them in the door. You close the door yourself.

---

## 7 · Test it before the post goes up

1. Comment `QUOTE` on the Reel from a second account, or have someone at the house do it.
2. Confirm: public reply appears, DM arrives, button works, link opens, and the sheet is viewable by someone who is not you.
3. Reply with a trade and confirm the tag lands.
4. Delete the test comment.

The most common failure is the link being set to Editor or restricted to your org, and you only find out after two hundred people have hit a permission wall. Open it in a private browser window while logged out. If it loads, you're good.

---

## 8 · If you don't have ManyChat working yet

Instagram's native comment-to-DM automation does most of this for free. It's in the app under the post's menu, or Professional Dashboard → Automated responses. It will do the keyword, the public reply and the first DM.

What it won't do is the button gate, the tags, the 24-hour follow-up, or the notification. So use it today so nothing is lost, and build the real flow in ManyChat this week.

**And if neither is ready when the post goes up:** put the link directly in the pinned comment and answer the DMs by hand. Two hundred manual DMs is a good problem and it's better than an automation that isn't live.
