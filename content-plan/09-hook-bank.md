# The Hook Bank

**120 hooks for tradesmen who want strangers to stop scrolling. Fill in the brackets, film the first frame, post.**

*From the 90-Day Speedrun Playbook · Version 1 · Sunday, September 13, 2026*

---

## How to use this

A hook is the first two seconds. It's the text on screen plus what the camera shows plus, if you're talking, the first sentence out of your mouth. All three land at once.

Three rules before you pick one:

1. **The first frame is never your logo, a slate, or you saying "hey guys."** It's the most extreme thing in the video, or the hook text on a clean frame.
2. **One hook, one idea, one post.** If the hook needs a second sentence to make sense, it's a caption, not a hook.
3. **Say it, then show it.** The visual should pay off the text within 3 seconds. "The moment the slab lets go" cuts to the slab letting go, not to you explaining the slab.

Every hook below has a fill-in-the-blank version and one worked example from a real trade. Swap in yours. Brackets are yours to fill.

Test any hook with three questions: *Would a stranger stop? Can I show it in 3 seconds? Is there one idea?* Three yeses, film it.

## 1 · The Moment (extreme visual, the thing they can't look away from)

Use when you have footage that's genuinely wild. The text just labels it.

1. The moment a [heavy thing] lets go.
   *Tree: The moment a 2,000 lb limb lets go, 60 feet up.*
2. [Number] [units] of [material], one [tool].
   *Concrete: 14 yards of concrete, one screed.*
3. [Distance] from [the thing you can't hit]. No room for a mistake.
   *Electrician: 8 inches from a live 480. No room for a mistake.*
4. What [X] pounds of [material] sounds like when it [lands / cracks / starts].
   *Roofer: What 900 pounds of shingles sounds like when it hits the dump trailer.*
5. POV: you get paid to do this.
   *Welder: POV: you get paid to do this. (arc on, close, no talking)*
6. This is what $[X] looks like from [here].
   *Tree: This is what $40,000 looks like from the top.*
7. [Start] to [finish] in [seconds] seconds.
   *Framer: Slab to roof in 40 seconds.*
8. The [quiet part of your job], no music.
   *Painter: Cutting in a ceiling line, no music.*
9. [Number] seconds that decide whether you go home.
   *Tree: The 3 seconds that decide whether you go home.*
10. Nobody tells you the [material] talks. Listen at [timestamp].
    *Tree: Nobody tells you the tree talks. Listen at 0:08.*
11. Watch the [thing] at [timestamp].
    *Mechanic: Watch the timing chain at 0:04.*
12. The part of the job the customer never sees.
    *Plumber: The part of the job the customer never sees. (crawlspace, headlamp)*

## The ten openers that kill a post

Delete the first two seconds if any of these are in them.

1. "Hey guys"
2. "So today we're going to…"
3. Your logo
4. A drone establishing shot with nothing happening
5. "Welcome back to the channel"
6. A slate or title card with music
7. You walking to the camera
8. "As you guys know…"
9. "I get asked a lot…"
10. A black frame or a fade-in
