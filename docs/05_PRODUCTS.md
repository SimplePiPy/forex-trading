# Products: what you sell after the service

Order matters. Services pay now. Content makes services warm. Products are built from what you already got paid to do.

## Already designed, open on schedule

**The 14-Day Sprint, $1,500, 5 seats, opens Day 21 (Oct 4).**
Two calls, fourteen days in their DMs, profile, keyword, first 30 posts built, one collab post. Curriculum: your Playbook Parts 2, 4 and 5, plus the Groundie series as the "build in public" module. Deliverable: their first seven posts live and keyword automation firing. Close the seats by Oct 10. Collect in full up front; Stripe payment link in the DM.

Make it better than every "content coaching" offer: you post their Day 1 declaration from your account as the collab. That is the thing nobody else can sell them.

**The 90-Day Climb, $3,700, 10 per group, opens Day 45 (Oct 28).**
Everything in the Sprint plus 12 weekly group calls, monthly 1:1s, three collabs, and the Crew Lead Board. Offer a 3-pay ($1,300 x 3). Sprint graduates get $1,500 credit. Four seats is a $14,800 month; ten is $37,000. Deliver the group calls on a Sunday evening right after your own review, so the review is the content for the call.

Add one line to the Climb: every member gets Groundie free for the 90 days. It costs you $12 a month per member, it makes the Climb about running a business and not just posting, and ten more installs become ten more testimonials.

## New, low effort, Day 30+

**The Speedrun Kit, $47, Gumroad.**
The 30 hooks (now 90), the ground shot list as a printable, the Sunday review sheet, the bio template, the first-7-days calendar, and three CapCut templates with your caption style. Gumroad takes 10%, no monthly fee. Offer it automatically to everyone who comments the keyword and does not reply to the Sprint pitch within 48 hours. Expect a few hundred a month at your current reach; it scales with followers and costs nothing after the first weekend.

**Claude Code skills and templates, $27 to $97, Gumroad.**
Package the Groundie repo as "the missed-call bot I run for tree companies" with the profile template, the audit generator, the ranker, and the install checklist. Buyers are the tradesmen in your audience who want to run it themselves, and the developers who follow build-in-public accounts. This is a real second channel for the same code.

## Groundie as software, month 3+

Once 10 clients are installed by hand, make it self-serve:
1. Stripe Checkout at $197/mo.
2. A form that writes the profile JSON (services, area, hours, policies, their voice sample).
3. Twilio number provisioning by API on signup, toll-free verification submitted automatically.
4. A `businesses` table keyed by the Twilio number so one deployment serves everyone.
5. The leads board gets a login.

That turns every Groundie post into a sale that does not need a call. At 25 clients the recurring base is $4,925 a month. At 50 it is $9,850, the $10k month floor with cohorts on top. Small SaaS at that size sells for 3 to 4x annual profit on Acquire.com, so a $5k/month Groundie is a $150k to $200k asset if you ever want out.

## The one you leave alone for now

"The First 30 Days" (men coming out of addiction, your mentorship) is a real asset and a different audience. Park it until the Climb is running. When you bring it back, it is a Quiet-pillar series and a $27 guide, not a pivot.

## Pricing discipline

- Sprint and Climb never go on sale. Seats are the scarcity.
- Groundie standard price never drops below $197. Founding-rate locks are the only discount.
- The Kit can be free for a weekend when a post hits a million views. That is the only promo.
