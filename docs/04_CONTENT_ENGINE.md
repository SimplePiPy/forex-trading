# Content engine: Instagram first, Facebook paid, Groundie in the story

Your playbook posts to Instagram first, then TikTok and YouTube. Facebook is not in it, and Facebook is the only platform paying you. Fix that first.

## 1. Cross-post Instagram to Facebook, automatically

Meta's Content Monetization Program pays the same RPM for AI-assisted content, demotes duplicates reposted within Facebook, and rewards volume (15 Reels across 10 days a month). Reels shared from Instagram through Meta's own sharing count as native on Facebook.

Setup, once, five minutes:
1. Accounts Center: add your Facebook Page and Instagram professional account to the same center.
2. Instagram: Settings, Accounts Center, Sharing across profiles. Turn on automatic sharing of Reels (and posts) to the Page.
3. Meta Business Suite: confirm the Page has Content Monetization enabled (it does, you were paid) and that Reels show in the monetization dashboard after your next post.
4. Meta Business Suite Planner is the fallback: schedule the Reel to both Instagram and Facebook from one upload, native on both.

What changes about the posts:
- **Slot B (12:30pm, Numbers or Business) goes 45 to 90 seconds.** Under 15 seconds gets no in-stream ads; 30 to 90 seconds qualifies for mid-roll and post-roll. Slot B is business and trades content, which is a higher RPM category than general entertainment. This slot is where Facebook money lives.
- Slot A and C stay short and loop. They are for reach and follows.
- Facebook captions can be longer. Put the hook, two lines, and the keyword. Facebook comment-to-DM automation does not exist natively; direct Facebook commenters to "message the page".

Math: at your current $134 a month, doubling monetized views through cross-posting and moving slot B to a mid-roll length is a plausible $300 to $500 a month inside 60 days with no extra filming.

## 2. The Groundie series (The Business pillar, 20% of posts)

You already film the work and the numbers. Add one thread: building and selling Groundie, in public. These are the posts that sell both the software and the Sprint, because the Sprint audience is watching a tradesman build a business on camera.

Series hooks (slot B unless noted):
1. "Day 19. I missed 11 calls this week because I was in a tree. Tonight I'm fixing that." (face, truck)
2. "I called myself and let it ring out. Watch what happened." (phone screen, slot A, sound on)
3. "I called 10 tree companies in Charlotte at 2pm. Here's how many picked up." (whiteboard or notes app)
4. "This is what a $1,500 job looks like when it goes to voicemail." (the industry stats, your math)
5. "First stranger's phone. First missed call. First text-back. He's watching." (install day, his face)
6. "What the text actually says. I didn't write it, I trained it on how I talk to customers." (screen recording of a real thread, name blurred)
7. "Week 1 numbers: 5 companies, 38 missed calls caught, 9 quote-ready, 2 jobs booked." (the board)
8. "The tree guy who told me his customers don't text." (story, confession formula)
9. "$197 a month vs one trim job. How I priced it." (The Business)
10. "How I got my first 3 paying software customers from a dump trailer." (Numbers, slot B, 60 to 90s, built for Facebook)
11. "Everybody watches the climber. This is who answers his phone now." (callback to your best hook)
12. "I'm a tree guy. I just shipped software. Day 33." (The Quiet, slot C)

Keyword for this series: GROUNDIE. Comment-to-DM reply: "Here's the 2-minute video. What trade are you?" Second message, 10 minutes later if they answer: the audit offer.

## 3. The weekly batch is now automatic

A Routine named "Sunday content batch (speedrun + Groundie)" runs every Sunday at 3:49pm Eastern in your Claude account. It drafts the week's 21 hooks across the three slots and four pillars with the Groundie series slotted in and Facebook lengths flagged, seven talking-head scripts, pinned comments, sequel ideas for last week's winner, the Sunday review checklist, and five audit reminders. You get a push notification and an email when it finishes; open the run and copy the batch into your Sunday review.

One limitation to know: the Routine was created from a coding session, and the platform would not attach Google Drive to it. So today it delivers the batch in its reply instead of saving a Google Doc. If you want it written straight into Drive as "Week N batch", recreate it from the Routines page on claude.ai with Google Drive connected and paste the same prompt (it is stored on the Routine). You can also edit the prompt, change the time, or pause it there.
